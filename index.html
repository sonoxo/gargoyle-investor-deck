The Threat

Small-Class Drones
Change the Threat Model

Cheap, quiet drones give bad actors an eye in the sky, a delivery path over the fence, and reconnaissance without ever touching the ground perimeter.

Robbery Support
Real-Time Eye in the Sky

Crews can scout entry points, watch guard patterns, and coordinate break-ins with live overhead visibility.

Corporate Espionage
Reconnaissance Without Access

Low-cost drones can observe yards, loading docks, prototypes, and executive movement without breaching a gate.

Payload Delivery
Carry Contraband or Devices

Even small drones can drop phones, narcotics, sensors, or malicious hardware onto otherwise secured property.

Executive & Event Risk
Stalking, Disruption, and Panic

At campuses, residences, or venues, a drone can surveil VIPs, disrupt operations, or trigger high-visibility security incidents.

The problem is not that the fence is broken. The problem is that the threat never needs to use the gate.
